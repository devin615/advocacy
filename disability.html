SSA Blue Book Strategy

Targeting Listing 11.04 (Stroke) and Listing 11.14 (Peripheral Neuropathy) for expedited approval.

Why These Listings?

Meeting a listing allows for approval at Step 3 without having to prove you can't do "any" work.

Listing 11.04: Vascular Insult

Covers residuals from 2024 lacunar strokes and 100% vertebral occlusion.

Listing 11.14: Neuropathy

Covers disorganization of motor function in two extremities (gait, balance, and grip).

Step 1: Evidence Preparation

SSA decisions rely on objective proof. Gather these immediately:

View Evidence Checklist
  • Imaging: CT/MRA showing occluded artery and infarcts.
  • Metabolic: Glucose logs (spikes to 211) and BUN:Crea ratios.
  • Neurology Notes: Documentation of sensory/motor loss and "extreme limitation" in walking/standing.
  • Functional Reports: Letters detailing the 140lb weight loss (cachexia) and falling risks.

Step 2: The Application

Apply as soon as possible to secure your "onset date" and back pay.

Fastest Method ssa.gov/applyfordisability
Phone Support 1-800-772-1213
Deadline Start Today

Step 3: Evaluation & Appeals

SSA follows a 5-step process. If denied (~70% of initial claims are), Appeal Immediately.

Appeal Tip: Most approvals happen at the Hearing level with an Administrative Law Judge. You have 60 days to appeal a denial.
