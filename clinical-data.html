2. The Vascular Blockage

Imaging confirms a 100% Left Vertebral Occlusion. David is surviving solely on "backup" vessels.

The Main Blockage 100% Blocked

Left Vertebral Artery

Plain English
One of the two main arteries that supplies blood to the back of your brain is completely shut. This is the most dangerous type of blockage because it forces your brain to rely on a "backup generator" (the Circle of Willis).
Thalamus Damage 0.9 cm Infarct

What is the Thalamus?
The Thalamus is like your brain's Main Switchboard. Damage here (the infarct) means sensory and motor signals are getting "dropped" or scrambled, leading to the numbness and coordination issues David is feeling.
Internal Capsule 0.6 cm Infarct

What this means
This is the Main Highway for messages between your brain and your limbs. Damage here directly affects physical movement and strength, especially on one side of the body.
Hemodynamic Status Ultra-Critical

The brain has no margin for error. Any dip in pressure or spike in heart rate (like 165 BPM) will cause the backup system to fail.
