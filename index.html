Stroke Risk Pathway: How it Happens

These items show how metabolic damage leads to vascular blockage and final cardiac risk.

Step 1: Metabolic Damage (50:1 Ratio) Active Catabolic State

BUN: 50 | Creatinine: 1.0 | Ratio: 50.0

What does 50:1 mean?

1. Creatinine (The Filter): 1.0
Creatinine is a waste product from normal muscle use. Your level of 1.0 is perfectly normal, which means your kidneys are filtering your blood exactly as they should be.

2. BUN (The Waste): 50
BUN measures waste left over from breaking down protein. A level of 50 is more than double the normal range (9-23 mg/dL).

3. The Ratio: 50.0
Because kidneys are filtering fine but waste is still high, a 50.0 ratio (Normal: 7-25) is clinical proof of a catabolic state.

The "Clean Chimney" Metaphor: Your kidneys are like a clean chimney (Creatinine 1.0) they are wide open. However, the house is filling with smoke (BUN 50). The "fire" is your body burning its own muscle and fat for fuel.
